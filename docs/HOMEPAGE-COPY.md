## Copy Principles Applied

- **Benefits over features:** Every service card names the outcome, not the tool
- **Customer language:** "moving data around", "nothing should require them", "the real version"
- **Specificity:** "6 hours every Monday", "7am report", not "save significant time"
- **One primary CTA:** "Book Free Audit" — repeated at top, middle, end
- **No buzzwords used:** No "streamline", "optimize", "unlock", "game-changing"
- **Honest positioning:** "Tell you what we'd build and what it would save" — not a pitch
